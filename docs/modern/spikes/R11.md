## No bands in this capture

`r5_ss_ab.cap` shows no wide vertical bands of light on the asphalt:
- **By eye:** contact sheets of every 75th frame over 300-3375 show none.
- **Blind detector:** over all 3171 frames from 250 to 3420 it fired on 14 frames (method below), in 7 clusters at
  least 40 frames apart. Each cluster's strongest frame (1468, 2398, 1047, 2117, 1007, 2599, 2070) is 3-5 columns wide
  at one of two fixed x positions (72-75 and 222-227 of 320).
  - Viewed: 1047, 2117 and 2398 are lane markings and kerbs.
  - The strongest of the 14, frame 1468, is a blocky cyan texture artefact on the tunnel road (passed to lane C with
    the recorder artefacts).
  - 1007, 2070 and 2599 were not viewed. They are at the same columns.

The detector's rule: in the road region (rows 55-92 %, columns 10-90 %, HUD masked), a run of 3 or more columns whose
mean linear Y is at least 1.3 x a running median across a quarter of the width, and is above 1.3 x its row's median in
at least 60 % of the rows.

So the draws below are described, not yet tied to the bands. The first run on a capture with bands should do step 1 of
RISKS R11 (bisection on a band frame) and the caller log, and also find target 37's producer (below).

## The lit-road layer: draws 305-308 (VBlanks 15774 and 15782, scene target 4)

The draw numbers are positions in the frame list, as `GT4_GSN_LIMIT` counts them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Draws | 305-308, consecutive, the same state; textures 21177, 21176, 21177, 21176 (host ids); 120, 201, 180, 252 vertices |
| Primitive | modeled triangle strips: textured, Gouraud, fogged, blended (PRIM 0x07C) |
| Blend | (Cs - Cd) As + Cd: an alpha blend by the vertex / texel alpha; no alpha test |
| Depth | z test GEQUAL, no z write: drawn onto the road surface already in the depth buffer |
| Target | scene target 4 (640 x 448, bp 0x1A40) |
| Batches (306-308; draw 305's not listed) | jobs 766-772 (308: 770-772), packet sources 011acbd0, 011ad1b0, 011ad6a0, 011adc80, 011adea0, 011ae4e0, 011aeb10; renderers 1 and 4; lighting body 0 (no lighting chain) |
| Coverage | draw 308 alone writes 69070 / 65801 of the road region's 69120 pixels (lower 40 %, centre 60 %), 120764 / 115702 of the target's |
| Lift | draw 308 raises the road's mean linear Y from 0.00346 to 0.00867 (15774) and from 0.00364 to 0.00693 (15782) |
| Destination alpha | unchanged by draws 306-308: 128 on the road region before draw 306 and after draw 308 |

**What it looks like.** Imaged alone (the frame after draw 308 minus the frame before it), draw 308 lights the whole
road surface with an asphalt-grain texture. The light is strongest down the road ahead and falls off toward the car:
the screen band at rows 280-335 gets +12.8 (8-bit) at 15774, and rows 392-447 get +9.2. As the car moves the bright
part slides down the screen: 8 frames later the same bands get +4.8 and +10.4. So the light is world-fixed: it moves
with the road geometry, not with the screen. The batches have no lighting chain (body 0), so the light is in their
vertex colours and alpha and their textures, not computed from lights at run time. The likely reading is light baked
into the course's geometry, probably the lamps' pools. Nothing measured here ties it to the lamps' positions.
Draws 306, 307 and 309 write no pixels in the road region (elsewhere 306: 121-129 px, 307: 579-849 px, 309: 6448-7772
px). Draw 309 is not part of the layer: alpha test EQUAL 0x80, z write on, texture 22516 (the lamp glows, below).

**Why it is not simply removed.** If it is the lamps' baked light, removing it leaves the road dark here. With
textures 21176, 21177 and 22516 removed for the whole replay (draws 305-309: the layer's textures, and 309's 22516),
over frames 2500-2528 the road's level stays within -4.74..-4.99 (mean log luminance) instead of rising to -3.96 at
2509, and 29.5 % of the frame's pixels change at 2514. That it is wanted light and not an artefact needs the PCSX2
frames. Decided in PLAN.md N3 / F2: the layer is skipped exactly when the renderer draws the lamps' own light on the
road (F2 in `hybrid`, F3 in `modern`), so the road gets the lamps' light once, never twice and never dark. A `hybrid`
preset with F2 off keeps the layer, and `original` always keeps it.

## A light-image glow: draws 325-326

| | |
|---|---|
| Draw 325 | fills target 71 (64 x 96, bp 0x3A20) from target 37 (64 x 32, bp 0x3A40): 6 sprites, opaque, vertex colour 128 |
| Target 71's content | a blurred image of bright lights in its top-left 32 x 32, alpha 128 there and 0 elsewhere |
| Draw 326 | 60 sprites sampling target 71, vertex colour 64 (half intensity), blend Cs Ad + Cd: added, weighted by the scene's alpha |
| The weight | the scene's alpha is 0 in the sky, 33 or more on what is drawn below it, and 128 on the road region, so the glow goes on the ground and buildings, not the sky |
| Depth | z test ALWAYS, no z write |
| Coverage, lift | 67952 / 59590 road pixels; +0.00245 / +0.00079 linear Y on the mean over the road region (15774 / 15782; R10's +0.00249 / +0.00092 are per written pixel) |

Imaged alone, draw 326 adds a broad dim wash over the road with a darker diagonal swath. Its 63 % smaller lift per
written pixel at 15782 mostly follows its source: target 71's colour sum falls from 102512 to 46733 (-54 %) and its
lit pixels from 507 to 348 (-31 %); target 37's mean falls from 17.2 to 8.4. Its weight does not change. The source's
fall leaves 9 points of the 63 % unexplained.

Sprites stretching a small image of the lights onto the ground are the shape RISKS R11 names for the road reflection
pass (`render_reflection` 003411D0). That makes draw 326 the first candidate for the bands once band frames exist.
The candidate is not confirmed: in this capture it makes no bands.

**Target 37's producer** (F2b step 0, same frame, VBlank 15774): the light image is a small 3D render of course
geometry, not a copy of the scene. Target 71 is cleared (draw 312), and course meshes are drawn into it in 3D (draws
313-320: four textures, alpha blend, alpha test GREATER 127, z on): the road-reflection view (`render_reflection`
003411D0, `kViewReflection`). Then it is blurred 71 -> 37 -> 71 -> 37 -> 71 (draws 322-325, 6 sprites each) and spread
over the road by draw 326. So draw 326 does not read the scene picture and is not one of R9's readers: in `hybrid` it
shows GT4's own reflection of its lamps. Whether N3 drops it stays with the band question.

## The street-lamp glows: draw 309 (F2b step 0)

The lamps light nothing in GT4 (A7: they are in none of the game's light lists), but their heads are drawn: the
star-shaped glows along the road and the floodlights are one modeled draw a frame onto the scene target, draw 309
(texture 22516 in R11's replay, 21426 in this one: host texture ids differ between runs).

| | |
|---|---|
| State | blend (Cs - Cd) As + Cd, alpha test EQUAL 128 (the glow's core writes z), z test GEQUAL with z write |
| Batches | renderer 3, lighting body 0, textured; 11 model jobs, 696 vertices, about 116 quads |
| Alone | skipping its texture removes every lamp glow along the road and the floodlight, and nothing else of note (22027 pixels at 1280 x 720, in blobs at the lamp heads) |
| Not it | the skyline backdrop (it holds no lamps), the bulbs' cores (ordinary opaque geometry), and the additive (`0201`) draws on the scene |

Found by a replay listing every draw's state, then skipping the scene's 317 textures' model jobs in halves against a
detector of the lamp dots (each dot's own brightness halving, since the backdrop behind a glow also changes), then
imaging the single texture's skip. So the lamp heads are separate geometry with a draw-state signature: PLAN F2b
finds their positions in the course meshes.
