# Spike R3: coplanar and alpha ordering

Status: the measurement is done on `race_boot.cap` (day: the event screen, the flyover, the grid and the race),
2026-11-08, lane B; the decision below is proposed for the architect. Question and method: RISKS.md R3. The night
routes and the showroom are not in this capture (open, section 4).

## 1. Classes (from R5's tooling)

The race's scene target, a frame (R5 section 2): about 627 tagged model runs. Most are opaque: Z test GEQUAL, Z
write, alpha test GEQUAL 0x20. About 90 are alpha-blended (`(Cs - Cd) As + Cd`), with or without Z write. About 14
are the car paint's reflection pass (destination alpha test, fixed blend, Z test without write, sampling the
environment map). The rest are R5's clears, Z-only draws and full-screen sprites.

## 2. Equal-depth overlap

A local probe (never pushed) drew every draw with a GEQUAL Z test twice before itself, writing nothing, under
occlusion queries: once with GREATER, once with GEQUAL. The difference is the pixels that pass only because their
Z equals what is there: the layers that rely on draw order. Totals over 3,000 frames:

| Draws (scene target unless said) | Draws | Pixels at equal Z |
|---|---|---|
| Opaque, Z write, alpha test (the course and the cars) | 525,097 | 3.0 % |
| Blended, Z write | 29,812 | 21.0 % |
| Blended, no Z write | 79,023 | 2.6 % |
| Opaque, no Z write | 14,097 | 2.5 % |
| Another target (t5): a second textured pass with a fixed blend, no Z write | 4,360 | 100 % |
| Another target (t59, likely an environment face): opaque, Z write | 49,892 | 9.7 % |

The probe's counts are wrong for passes with the destination alpha test (its stencil passes): those rows are left
out (the paint pass is known to be drawn over the body at equal Z: NATIVE_RENDERER.md).

So most pixels do not depend on order, but no class is free of it: coplanar layers exist among the opaque draws
(3 %, road markings and decals over the road), the blended ones with Z write rely on it most (21 %), and GT4 draws
second passes over a surface at equal Z (100 %).

## 3. Proposed decision

- **B7 (parity) draws in GT4's order, forward, with GT4's depth.** Each instance through its own projection
  (`InstanceData::projection`, a.8: "parity and GS Z"), the Z test as GT4 sets it, its Z clears between partitions
  kept as the view's passes. Its depth then equals the GS path's, ties resolve as they do there, and all of section
  2 is reproduced without a class rule. No G-buffer in B7.
- **B10 and B22 (the modern lighting) need the split**, since they use the view's own reverse-Z depth, where GT4's
  ties no longer tie: opaque draws with Z write into the G-buffer, in GT4's order (ties among them broken by order
  and a depth bias for a run drawn at equal Z over an earlier one); blended draws, draws without Z write and second
  passes at equal Z (the paint's reflection, multipass textures) in an ordered forward pass after the lighting,
  keeping GT4's order. Which runs are "at equal Z over an earlier one" is per material (B6's table can carry the
  bit, measured once per material as here).
- RISKS.md R3's prototype of the two orders belongs to B10 (G-buffer against forward), not to B7.
