# Spike R12: where the full-frame yellow tint from warning lights comes from

Status: **closed for its box: no full-frame tint found** (2026-11-08). The owner is away, so the coordinator set a box
of three live sessions shared with [R11](R11.md). The only blinking warning lights found, at the pit entry of
S.S. Route 5 Normal at night, do not shift the colour of the rest of the frame (a luminance change of at most
0.41 %, correlated with the lamp in the hood view, and no colour shift). N4 goes ahead on the mechanism (ARCHITECTURE.md
section 8: the flare pass and the glare are skipped in `modern`). Measured on `main` f33e4a8 (game and host), live
frames, no instrumentation. Questions: [RISKS.md R12](../RISKS.md).

## The lights found

S.S. Route 5, **Normal** direction, night, Arcade time trial (Ford Ka): with the accelerator held and no steering, the
car slid along the walls from the start straight to the pit entry and stopped against the pit sign (the session in
R11.md, "The search", item 3). There a signal of two yellow lamps, one above the other, blinks: the lamps light
alternately, about once a second.

## Measurement

Live frames at 640 x 360, bursts of 600 frames, the car at rest. Per burst:
- **The lamp:** the 0.3 % of scene pixels whose luminance varies most over the burst. Their mean is the lamp signal, and
  its zero crossings give the blink period.
- **The rest of the frame:** the scene (HUD masked) without the lamp pixels, dilated by 6 px. Its mean linear Y and mean
  OKLab b (yellow +) are compared between lamp-on and lamp-off frames (a median split of the lamp signal) and
  correlated with the signal.

| Burst | View | Blink period | Lamp pixels' swing | Rest: Y on/off | Rest: OKLab b on - off | corr(lamp, b) | corr(lamp, Y) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L8 | hood | 60 frames | 350 % | +0.36 % | +0.0000 | +0.01 | +0.49 |
| L9 | in-car | 54 frames | 1412 % | +0.31 % | -0.0001 | -0.05 | +0.13 |
| L10 | in-car | 54 frames | 1277 % | -0.41 % | -0.0003 | -0.22 | -0.07 |
| L11 | chase | 60 frames | 68 % | +0.02 % | +0.0000 | +0.01 | +0.04 |
| L12 | chase | 60 frames | 70 % | +0.02 % | +0.0000 | -0.00 | +0.03 |

So the frame outside the lamps shows no colour shift with the blink (OKLab b at most 0.0003, correlations -0.22 to
+0.01). Its luminance changes by at most 0.41 %, and in the hood view it follows the lamp (corr +0.49, +0.36 % on/off;
in-car L9 +0.13): a little glow, well below a visible tint.
L7 (the car still settling) and the bursts while driving (L1-L6) are left out, because there the variance is the
motion, not a lamp.
