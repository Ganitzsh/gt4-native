# Spike R10: where the Route 5 section brightness step comes from

Status: **closed** (2 of 3 days), decided from the data: the owner is away and the coordinator closed it without
their location. Measured 2026-11-07 on `main` 48da2b6 + local instrumentation (not committed), on one capture of
S.S. Route 5 **Reverse** at night (`r5_ss_ab.cap`, local only, 3600 frames from the B-spec race; CAPTURES.md).
Questions: [RISKS.md R10](../RISKS.md).

## Conclusion

**On S.S. Route 5 Reverse at night there is no one-frame section step in the game's output as this host draws it
(GS path; the PCSX2 comparison is still open).** The four places where
the frames' level changes at a fixed track position (replay frames 507, 1049, 2514 and 2859) are **changes that build
over 8-20 frames**: N1 15-frame levels of 9.8-31.0 % (the scene's level 7.0-21.1 %, the road's 13.1-50.1 %). At each
of them the largest one-frame change of the whole scene is 3.0-4.6 %.

What changes:
- At 2514, the largest, about two thirds of the road's change is the lit-road layer the car drives out of: draw 308
  of that frame (the layer is draws 305-308, [R11](R11.md)), and probably draw 326, which was not ablated. The
  layer is world-fixed (it slides down the screen as the car moves) and has no lighting chain. The likely reading is
  light baked into the course, probably the lamps' pools.
- No grading, fog colour or batch lighting value switches the picture at 507 and 2514. The glare was not checked,
  and 1049 and 2859 only partly.
- The grading's curve alpha follows the picture's level 1-2 frames later (R9's luminance probe); it does not drive it.

**For N2.** No source switches in one frame here, so there is nothing for N2 part a (blending `kGrade` / fog
changes) to smooth on this course. The changes are most likely lighting baked into the course passing under the
car. In `hybrid` they are probably wanted light (R11; to be confirmed against PCSX2). In `modern` the clustered lights replace GT4's lamp light (ARCHITECTURE.md
section 8), so they go with it.

Open, and not blocking: the owner's own location for "one step" (not available), the glare, and a PCSX2 comparison.

**The R11 / R12 sessions** (2026-11-08, [R11](R11.md) "The search") were checked with PLAN.md N2's reopening test. No
one-frame step was found, so N2 stays parked:
- **Clubman Stage Route 5 Reverse at night** (`r11_csr5r.cap` replayed, frames 300-10990). N1 reports 40 level shifts.
  Eleven pass the 70 % one-frame share. All eleven are at camera changes: the menus (715, 1751), the start
  sequence's cameras (2306-2537, 3014), and the view changes made by hand (3662, 4043, 4546, 4850, 8589). In the
  fixed views (bumper about 4900-8589, chase from 8589) none passes. The test also leaves out frames right after a
  replay stall (a repeated frame), which shows several game frames' change at once. On `r5_ss_ab.cap` that removed
  one false hit (3196, after 8 repeated frames).
- **S.S. Route 5 Normal at night**: only the start straight to the pit entry was driven, about a minute. Its largest
  one-frame change while driving is 6.7 % (burst L5, frame 188; not examined), with the car sliding along the walls.
  The sections were not crossed, so the Normal direction is not covered.

## Ruled out (on Reverse, where each item says)

- **`kGrade` grading.** The weights are 0x80 on the diagonal in every frame that has the record (3525; 75 have none,
  none at the four places), and the curve's RGB is the same in all of them: only its alpha changes (72 distinct curves, 203 changing frames, 184 of them in 300-3400). The GS's way
  (`func_004A81A8`) never weights by it: ALPHA_1 is (Cs - 0) FIX + Cd with FIX 0x80, the first pass unblended, FRAME_1
  and FRAME_2 with FBMSK 0xFF000000 (alpha kept), TEX0 TCC 1 (read, then unused). The host's pass
  (`FrameList::Effect`) ignores it too. One-frame scene changes at the 184 curve changes against the other 2854
  moving frames, lags -2..+2: median 1.0-1.2 % against 1.2 %, share above 3 % 9.3-15.2 % against 14.8-15.1 %.
  The curve alpha follows the frames' level 1-2 frames later at 1049 and 2859 (not at 507 and 2514): in R9's terms
  the luminance probe (00330AE8) reacting to the picture, not driving it.
- **Fog colour, at the four places.** FOGCOL is 0x0c0c0c in every frame around them. The fog factor (each vertex's F,
  or FOG) was not checked.
- **Fog colour switching elsewhere.** FOGCOL is 0x000000 in four runs away from the four places (frames 648-655,
  1613-1624, 3114-3122, 3265-3275). Its 8 edges (the first frame of each run and the first frame after it) make no
  one-frame step. Against ordinary moving frames (300-3400), at lags -2..+2:
  - **Horizon band** (rows 33-55 %; ordinary median 1.1 %, p90 3.3 %, p99 11.2 %): 0.3-3.4 % at lag 0, within the
    ordinary frames' p99 (one just above their p90). At most 5.2 % at other lags, inside ramps whose neighbouring
    frames move as much.
  - **Scene** (ordinary p90 3.6 %, p99 8.2 %): 0.2-6.9 % at lag 0, within the p99. The 6.9 % (3114) is inside a ramp
    of +7 % a frame from 3112.
  - **Sky** (ordinary p99 20.6 %): 9-18 % a frame for about 5 frames at both edges of run 2 (around 1613 and 1625).
    Each frame is within the sky's p99, but the change lines up with run 2's edges, which are also the lit tunnel's
    entry and exit. It is not separated from the tunnel: whether it starts at the portal or at the FOGCOL frame was
    not checked.
  - No camera cut (structure correlation 0.984-0.998, median 0.994). On screen: run 2 is in a lit tunnel; runs 1, 3 and 4 are on the open road with the
  same view before and inside the run. Whether the runs line up with the qwords 206-215 switching was not checked
  (they are logged at 15774 and 15782 only).
- **Light values in the batches, at 507 and 2514 only.** Qwords 201-216 held: step unchanged at both. Qwords 205-208
  held at 507: unchanged. Qwords 206-215 frozen at 2514: no visible effect. At 1049 and 2859 they are not ruled out.
- **Draw 328 and the lighting body 0x1508, at 2514:** removing either leaves the step.
- **Coverage, at 2514:** draw 308 keeps 95.3 % of its road pixels (65801 of 69070), draw 326 87.7 % (59590 of 67952),
  against falls of 34 % and 63 % in their lift per pixel.
- **What else the game sends at 507 and 2514** (the earlier frame-by-frame comparison across each): the model packets,
  the steady constants, VU1 entries, texture sets (49 steady, 6 sent every frame), the draws that are not 3D batches,
  and the draws' textures and states are the same on both sides. The exceptions are the instances that come into
  view, such as draw 328's.

## For A11 and R11

- **A per-section signal.** Qwords 206-215 of the batches are one block per group of draws, 12 distinct blocks per frame,
  shared by every job of a group whatever its renderer or body. At 2514 every group switches its block in the same
  frame. For example the near group (draws 296-332, 143 / 159 jobs) goes q207 13.07, 14.20, 11.36 -> 7.36, 8.00,
  6.40; q208 3.90 -> 5.28; q212 31.7 -> 41.1; q213 18.8 -> 9.6 (red channel; the same tint), and q210 / q211 change
  too. No batch writes them with a batch write (only qwords 193-200 and 201-203), so they reach VU1 memory another
  way. They do not change the picture, but they may be what A11 can read as the section index, or a check for it.
  Checked at 2514 only. Lane A has the note.
- **R11's starting draws.** The lit overlay at 2514 is draws 306-309 (textures 21176, 21177, 22516; jobs 766-783,
  sources 011acbd0, 011ad1b0, 011ad6a0, 011adc80, 011adea0, 011ae4e0, 011aeb10 plus 11 renderer-3 batches without a
  model packet) and draw 326 (target 71's texture times destination alpha). Not a projected-light pass: their body
  is 0, not 0x1608.

## Method

Local patches, never committed: a per-frame log in `gs_frontend.cpp` (the `kGrade` record, fog, batch and draw
counts, per-draw state and vertex colours for a VBlank range, each modeled draw's jobs with renderer and body, and
each job's ModelJob and 1024 words); in `stream.cpp`, per-run batch writes, model packets and texture sets for a
VBlank range. Switches for the ablations:
- hold qwords at their pre-FROM values at each batch's run, optionally only for given packet sources;
- freeze qwords per job, keyed by renderer, chain, counts and strip header;
- drop model jobs by lighting body or texture.

Replays of `r5_ss_ab.cap` headless with `--preset original`, `display.frame_interpolation=off`,
`texture.hd_pack=off`: every frame at 320 x 180 for the series, single frames at 640 x 448 for the target dumps
(`GT4_GSN_DUMP`, `GT4_GSN_LIMIT`). Draw K is the K-th Draw command of the frame list, as `GT4_GSN_LIMIT` counts.
Job k of a frame is its k-th batch run (1313 / 1329 of each at 15774 / 15782).
